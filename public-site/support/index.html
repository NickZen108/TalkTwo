Sign-in help

TalkTwo uses passwordless email sign-in. If a sign-in link does not arrive, confirm the email address, check spam/junk, request a new link, and open the newest link on the device where you want to use TalkTwo. Never forward a TalkTwo sign-in link to another person.

Messages and communication windows

A message may be sent before it becomes available to the recipient. Each recipient controls their own communication windows and may also choose to check waiting messages manually.

TalkTwo does not provide automatic read receipts. Delivery information shown to a sender is aggregate delivery-to-app status and does not reveal who opened a message or when it was opened.

Sensitive messages and blocking

TalkTwo may mark a message as potentially conflict-escalating. Depending on the message and plan, a recipient may reject a flagged message without opening it. Blocking is private, and ordinary delivery status is designed not to reveal that a recipient has blocked the sender.

TalkTwo's communication review is a product feature, not professional medical, legal or crisis advice.

Premium and purchases

Consumer purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. Store-displayed localized prices and billing periods are authoritative. If a purchase appears missing after reinstalling or changing devices, use TalkTwo's restore-purchases flow before contacting support.

Deleting a TalkTwo account does not cancel an active store subscription. Cancel the subscription in the relevant Apple or Google store account to stop future billing.

Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account in TalkTwo under Account & privacy → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, use the authenticated public deletion flow.
