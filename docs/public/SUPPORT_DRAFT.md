## Sign-in help

TalkTwo uses passwordless email sign-in. If a sign-in link does not arrive:

- check that the email address is correct;
- check spam/junk folders;
- request a new sign-in link;
- open the newest link on the device where you want to use TalkTwo.

Never forward a TalkTwo sign-in link to another person.

## Messages and communication windows

A message may be sent before it becomes available to the recipient. Each recipient controls their own communication windows. A recipient can also choose to check waiting messages manually.

TalkTwo does not provide automatic read receipts. Delivery information shown to a sender is aggregate delivery-to-app status and does not reveal who opened a message or when it was opened.

## Sensitive messages

TalkTwo may mark a message as potentially conflict-escalating. Depending on the message and plan, the recipient may be able to reject a flagged message without opening it.

TalkTwo's communication review is a product feature, not professional medical, legal or crisis advice.

## Blocking

Blocking is private. Future messages from a blocked person remain unreadable to the blocker. TalkTwo is designed so ordinary delivery status does not reveal that a recipient has blocked the sender.

## Premium and purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play for consumer purchases. Store-displayed localized prices are authoritative.

Deleting a TalkTwo account does not cancel an active store subscription. Cancel a subscription in the relevant Apple/Google store account to stop future billing.

If a purchase appears missing after changing devices or reinstalling, use TalkTwo's store restore flow before contacting support.

## Account deletion

Signed-in users can delete their account under **Account & privacy → Delete account**.

Before Google Play submission, TalkTwo will also provide the external authenticated deletion flow at `https://talktwo.app/delete-account`.
