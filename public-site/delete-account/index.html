Permanent deletion

Signed in as .

Deleting your TalkTwo account removes your account, profile, chat memberships, settings and server-side message data involving your account. It also removes local decrypted messages and conversation keys from the device where deletion is performed by the app.

Other conversation participants may still have messages they already opened and stored on their own devices. TalkTwo cannot remotely erase private data stored on somebody else's device.

Deleting TalkTwo does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel the subscription in the relevant store to stop future billing.

This is permanent. A new account using the same email will not recover deleted chats or encryption keys.
